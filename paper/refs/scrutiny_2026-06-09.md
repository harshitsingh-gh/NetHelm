## VERDICT: the idea is novel and on-trend; the FRAMING must change and the SCIENCE must get done.

### What's confirmed RIGHT (keep)
- **Moat intact (re-scanned live).** No benchmark scores an LLM's *predicted, typed,
  false-positive/hallucinated* damage from a static config diff. NetArena (2506.03231)
  = agent *action* safety; NIKA (2512.16381) = diagnosis/RCA; NetConfBench (draft-01)
  = synthesis correctness. The 2026 agentic-NetOps survey (**arXiv:2605.12729**) names
  the exact gap: "predictive blast-radius estimation for LLM proposals remains
  underspecified." NIKA itself lists Batfish-validated remediation prediction as
  *future work* — the closest competitor confirms the gap.
- **Infrastructure is publication-grade:** Batfish-static oracle, pinned digest, ≥10×
  determinism gate, scenario-level stats, contamination-control generator, prompt-
  hygiene CI lint, the false-session-alarm-rate thesis metric. Stats match 2026 norms.
- **12 hand-authored mechanisms are a credible, citable spread** at the mechanism level.

### THE TWO CHANGES (panel converged)
1. **REFRAME the narrative (cheap, decisive) — from "no-tools static blast-radius
   prediction" to "the verification-wall pre-gate / autonomy-ladder rung-2→3
   graduation test."** Sell: *can an LLM predict the blast radius of its OWN proposed
   change well enough to be trusted to propose changes — and does it false-alarm?*
   This answers "why no tools?" (you're probing the model's internal world-model,
   which determines whether a verifier can be the backstop vs the only net) and rides
   the agentic trend. Cite 2605.12729's gate/autonomy-ladder language.
2. **ADD a tools-allowed contrast (the highest-leverage experiment) + RUN THE SWEEP.**
   Run scenarios in conditions: (a) no-tools predict (current), (b) tools-allowed
   (model may issue Batfish/show queries), and report the GAP. This: kills the
   skeptical-VP one-liner ("why an LLM that guesses what Batfish computes?"), makes
   the paper about "tools necessary AND insufficient" (a HotNets-shaped insight),
   inoculates against the scoop, and makes the determinism rigor load-bearing for
   grading BOTH conditions. Keep the tools arm small (the 12 hand-authored scenarios).

### THE DOMINANT EXECUTION GAP (all four lenses, blunt)
**No real sweep exists. The only evidence is 4 single-shot Haiku smokes that FLOOR at
the empty baseline** — which cannot yet distinguish "LLMs lack the skill" (thesis)
from "the task is impossible for everyone" (non-discriminating, fatal). The project
keeps authoring scenarios when the binding constraint is the sweep. **Stop building
scenarios. Run ≥4 frontier models (incl. a non-Anthropic for cross-vendor) × N≥5 ×
the splits × the tool conditions.** This is the entire scientific payload.
